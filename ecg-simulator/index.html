        How it works: each beat is built as a 3-D cardiac dipole (P, QRS, ST, T and U components) and projected onto real lead vectors, so axis changes, reciprocal ST changes and lead reversals follow from geometry rather than being drawn per lead. Timing comes from an event model of the sinus node, AV node (refractory period with decremental recovery and concealed conduction), His–Purkinje system, accessory pathway and ventricular foci. The ladder diagram is drawn from the same events. QTc uses Bazett. This is a teaching model, not a diagnostic tool.
