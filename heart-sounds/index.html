Controls

72 bpm
Rhythm
3 / 6
Severity
Shorter A2–opening snap interval and a longer rumble mean higher left atrial pressure.
Manoeuvre
Smaller LV volume (standing, Valsalva) moves the click earlier and lengthens the murmur.
Chest piece
The diaphragm brings out high-pitched sounds (MR, clicks); the bell, held lightly, brings out low-pitched ones (MS rumble, S3).
